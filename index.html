      The worlds

      1. Rural town: dairy, post shop, courier vans, tractors
      2. Wellington: the Beehive, cable car, the hillside sign. Watch for southerlies.
      3. Hospital wing: patients, cleaners and maintenance staff about
      4. School grounds: prefabs, playgrounds, teachers and kids
      5. Industrial plant: asbestos everywhere, debris flying, chunks falling from the ceiling

      Three sites per world. Site 3 ends in a boss fight. Clear all five and the loop starts again, harder.

      Field guide

      Chrysotile
      White asbestos · 100
      Amosite
      Brown asbestos · 150
      Crocidolite
      Blue asbestos · 200
      Removal monster
      Two hits up close or one thrown · 250
      Bystanders
      Leave them alone. Hitting one costs 200.
      Scuba mask
      5 seconds of immunity. Anything hostile you touch is destroyed.
      Site audit
      The SQN auditor runs out to meet you early on the first site of every world. Answer right for +10% on that site's points, wrong for −10%. In worlds 3 to 5 a right answer also gives a scuba mask or Super Vacuum until halfway.
      Hospital tower
      Site 3-1 is a five-storey hospital: clear each floor, then take the stairs up and head back the other way. The decon unit is on the top floor.
      Continue
      Once a game, when you run out of lives, answer a harder awareness question correctly for 3 extra lives.
      Lives
      Start with 4. A perfect site (no asbestos missed, no lives lost) earns an extra life, up to 5.
      Tool pickup
      +5 chisels, hammers or sprays. Stockpile up to 20, and you start each site with none.
