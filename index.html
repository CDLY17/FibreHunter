    The worlds

    1. Rural town: dairy, post shop, courier vans, tractors
    2. Wellington: the Beehive, cable car, the hillside sign. Watch for southerlies.
    3. Hospital wing: patients, cleaners and maintenance staff about
    4. School grounds: prefabs, playgrounds, teachers and kids
    5. Industrial plant: asbestos everywhere, debris flying, chunks falling from the ceiling

    Three sites per world. Site 3 ends in a boss fight. Clear all five and the loop starts again, harder.
